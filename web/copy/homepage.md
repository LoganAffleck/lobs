## Features

**Add the cam once. That's the feature.**
Every scene gets its own copy to resize, crop and filter. The webcam only gets captured once.

**Color grade once, not once per scene.**
Link a cam's filters, size or crop across scenes. Or don't. Your call.

**Circle cam in two clicks.**
Crop right on the preview, pick 1:1, slide the corners to 50%. Done.

**Draw on stream like a sports commentator.**
Boxes, arrows, text, a pencil, and a laser pointer that fades out on its own.

**Layers that work like Figma.**
Drag to reorder, Ctrl+G to group, Ctrl+] to bring forward.

**Hard to mess up mid-stream.**
Layers lock while you're live (hold Alt to move one). Ending the stream takes two clicks.

**Setup that tests your internet first.**
A 10 second test stream that doesn't go live, then it picks your bitrate.

## Questions

**Do I need OBS installed?** Nope. LOBS ships its own OBS 32.2.2 engine and leaves your OBS alone.

**Can I import my OBS scenes?** Yep. Scenes, sources, crops, main filters and audio come over. Groups get flattened and nested scenes get skipped.

**Do OBS plugins work?** Not yet. That includes obs-websocket.

**Why is Windows yelling at me?** The installer isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

**Is it free?** Yes. GPL, no account, no watermark.

**Mac? Linux?** Windows only for now.
